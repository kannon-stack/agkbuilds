# AGKBUILDS website outcomes

## Complete — Interactive personal hero and navigation

- The page identifies Dylan Borgen and AGKBUILDS in Texas.
- The hero feels playful but credible and reacts to the cursor through a subtle signal field and tilt response.
- The page exposes anchor navigation to work, about, experience, recognition, and contact.

## Complete — Bento-grid about section

- The page includes an asymmetrical bento-grid about section with concise positioning copy, operating principles, and capability signals.

## Complete — Six selected projects

- The page presents six selected projects.
- Each project includes a short write-up and clear metadata/category treatment.

## Complete — Experience timeline

- The page includes a readable experience timeline with role, scope, geography, and time framing.

## Complete — Talks and awards

- The page includes a talks and awards / recognition section with distinct entries and status labels.

## Complete — Contact block

- The page ends with a contact block and a direct email CTA.

## Complete — Interactive terminal easter egg

- Visitors can open a custom terminal with the `~` or `/` keyboard shortcut or the fixed terminal trigger.
- The terminal accepts commands including `help`, `whoami`, `background`, `skills`, `experience`, `work`, `contact`, and `clear`.
- Responses are grounded in Dylan Borgen's résumé and match the matrix visual language.

## Complete — Custom Matrix rain background

- The website includes a custom canvas-based Matrix rain layer behind the portfolio content.
- The animation uses responsive resizing, subtle green glyph trails, and a reduced-motion guard.

## Complete — Managed website delivery hygiene

- The page is served from `/`; 404-only fallback routes are excluded from the application route manifest.
- `public/manus-routes.json` describes the complete route set.
- TypeScript diagnostics remain registered and the static build passes.
